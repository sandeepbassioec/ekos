# 🎯 Create Cloudinary Upload Preset - EXACT STEPS

## Follow These Steps EXACTLY (Don't Skip Any!):

### Step 1: Go to Settings
1. Open: https://console.cloudinary.com/
2. Click the **gear icon (⚙️)** in the TOP RIGHT corner
3. You'll see a left sidebar with options

### Step 2: Click Upload Tab
1. In the LEFT SIDEBAR, click **"Upload"**
2. NOT "Account", NOT "Security" - click **"Upload"**

### Step 3: Find Upload Presets Section
1. Scroll DOWN the page
2. You'll see a section called **"Upload presets"**
3. Below it, there's a link that says **"Add upload preset"**
4. Click **"Add upload preset"**

### Step 4: Fill The Form EXACTLY
A form will appear. Fill it like this:

**Upload preset name:**
```
esip_documents
```
(Type exactly this, no spaces, no capital letters)

**Signing Mode:** 
- Click the dropdown
- Select **"Unsigned"** ← MUST BE UNSIGNED!
- DO NOT select "Signed"

**Folder (optional):**
```
esip/documents
```

**Leave everything else as default!**

### Step 5: Save
1. Scroll to bottom of form
2. Click **"Save"** button
3. You should see a success message

### Step 6: Verify
After saving, you should see your preset in the list:
```
Upload preset name: esip_documents
Signing mode: Unsigned
Folder: esip/documents
```

---

## If You Can't Find "Upload" Tab:

Direct link to Upload settings:
```
https://console.cloudinary.com/settings/upload
```

---

## After Creating Preset:

1. Wait 10 seconds (for it to activate)
2. Refresh your browser
3. Try upload again

It will work!
