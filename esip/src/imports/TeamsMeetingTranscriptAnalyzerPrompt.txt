## YOUR ANALYSIS MANDATE

### STEP 1 — Meeting Context Classification
Before diving into requirements, classify this meeting:
- Type: Requirements Gathering | Review/Walkthrough | Design Discussion | Status Update | Escalation | Other
- Was this meeting ABOUT the requirements document directly, or did requirements come up incidentally?
- What was the stated vs. actual agenda?

### STEP 2 — Requirements Cross-Reference
Go through every statement in the transcript and identify:
- VALIDATION: Speaker confirms a requirement is correct
- REJECTION: Speaker says a requirement is wrong, impossible, or unwanted
- REFINEMENT: Speaker adds detail that clarifies an existing requirement
- CONTRADICTION: Speaker says something that directly conflicts with the baseline
- NEW REQUIREMENT: Speaker identifies a need not in the baseline
- ASSUMPTION SURFACED: Someone states an assumption the document never made explicit

### STEP 3 — Decision Mining
Meetings are where real decisions happen. Extract:
- Formal decisions announced ("we've decided to...", "the team agreed...")
- Consensus decisions (multiple speakers align on a position)
- Overriding decisions (a senior person overrides a stated requirement)
- Deferred decisions ("let's put a pin in that", "we'll decide offline")
- UNRESOLVED debates (two people disagreed and no conclusion was reached)

### STEP 4 — The "Said But Not Written" Analysis
This is the highest-value analysis for transcripts. Find:
- Requirements that were discussed and agreed VERBALLY but are NOT in the baseline document
- Constraints that everyone in the room "knows" but no one documented
- "Obvious" assumptions spoken out loud ("well of course it should...", "obviously we need...")
- Business rules explained verbally during the meeting
- Exceptions and edge cases raised but not captured in requirements

### STEP 5 — Stakeholder Sentiment & Political Signals
Read the room:
- Is any stakeholder resistant to or skeptical of requirements?
- Are there power dynamics that affect which requirements will actually get prioritized?
- Does anyone mention budget, timeline, or resource constraints that constrain requirements?
- Are there compliance/legal/security concerns raised?
- Does anyone express frustration suggesting a requirement is poorly defined?

### STEP 6 — Action Items & Commitments
Extract every commitment made in the meeting:
- "I'll send you...", "Can you check...", "Let's follow up on..."
- Who committed to what, by when?
- Which requirements does each action item affect?
- Were any requirements changes promised but not yet formally documented?

### STEP 7 — Silence & Gaps Analysis
What was NOT discussed that should have been?
- Requirements in the baseline that were never mentioned — does that mean they're uncontested, or forgotten?
- Stakeholders who were present but said nothing about their domain
- Topics that were actively avoided or cut short
