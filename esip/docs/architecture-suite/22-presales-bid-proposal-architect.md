## BID-01 — Persona Definition & Journey {#bid-01}

### BID-01-A: Persona Profile

The **Presales / Bid / Proposal Architect** (P14) is the practitioner who owns the end-to-end response to a competitive or sole-source procurement event — an RFP (Request for Proposal), RFI (Request for Information), RFQ (Request for Quotation), RFR (Request for Response), ITT (Invitation to Tender), or equivalent. The persona spans two organisational archetypes:

1. **Presales / Solution Sales Architect** (technology vendors, SIs, consultancies) — shapes the proposed solution, writes win themes, authors the technical response, and prices the engagement to win the deal.
2. **Bid / Tender Architect** (public-sector, infrastructure, defence, NHS, government) — responds to formal tenders under procurement regulations (PCR 2015, FAR/DFARS, EU Directives), manages mandatory compliance, coordinates a multi-company consortium response.

Both archetypes share the same ESIP capability set but differ in mandatory compliance rigour and pricing governance.

**Key activities in the Shipley bid-process lifecycle:**
- Opportunity identification → bid/no-bid decision → opportunity qualification (MEDDIC/BANT/Challenger)
- Kickoff → responsibility assignment (RACI matrix per section)
- RFP parse → compliance matrix → storyboard → win theme validation
- Parallel section authoring (executive summary, technical, commercials, CVs, references)
- Internal review (Pink Team → Red Team → Gold Team) → final production
- Submission → customer clarification → BAFO (Best and Final Offer) if applicable
- Win/loss debrief → knowledge capture → answer bank update

**Persona pain points (present state without ESIP):**
- RFP documents are 50–500-page PDFs; manual cross-referencing takes days
- Answer bank is a shared SharePoint folder of inconsistent Word fragments
- No automated compliance tracking → missed mandatory items risk disqualification
- Pricing assembled from ten disconnected spreadsheets; no audit trail
- Multiple contributors with no concurrent editing → version-control chaos
- No win/loss data tied to specific answer choices → no learning loop
