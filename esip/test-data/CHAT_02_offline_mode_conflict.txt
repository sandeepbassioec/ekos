Ahmed Hassan [10:05] Has anyone seen the feedback from the field pilot in Region East?
Jake shared a summary: 23 out of 40 pilot technicians reported they go through tunnels or
basements regularly where they have zero connectivity for 10-15 minutes at a time.
In those windows, the current app just shows a spinner. Work order updates are lost.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Lakshmi Reddy [10:09] Yes I saw that. This is the offline mode problem that was explicitly
put "out of scope" in the PRD. Section 3.2 says "Offline-first mobile support — under evaluation, not committed".

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ahmed Hassan [10:11] Right, but here's the thing — with v2.0 we're adding real-time location
tracking and live ETA updates. If the technician goes offline for 15 minutes and can't push
location pings, the dispatcher's map shows them as "lost" — the ETA goes stale, the SLA
dashboard flags their job as at-risk even if the tech is making good progress.

So v2.0 actually MAKES the offline problem WORSE than it is today, because v1.4 doesn't
show ETAs at all — it just shows last status. v2.0 promises live ETAs but can't deliver
them during connectivity gaps. Customers will see "SLA At Risk" alerts that aren't real.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Lakshmi Reddy [10:17] That's a really important point. We're raising the bar on what the
system promises and then not being able to deliver it in a common real-world scenario.
What's the gap between "lost connection" and "SLA alert fires"?

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ahmed Hassan [10:20] Looking at the logic: location ping expected every 30s (Travelling)
or 300s (In Progress). If we miss 4 consecutive pings (2 min for Travelling) the location
service marks the tech as "location unknown". The dashboard shows that as amber.
If unknown >5 min it turns red. So a 15-min tunnel means: the tech is shown red for
13 minutes. Any Critical or High SLA job they hold will trigger re-assignment attempts.
The tech could come out of the tunnel to find their jobs have been reassigned.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Lakshmi Reddy [10:24] That's genuinely bad. Not a future problem — this will happen on
day 1 with Region East technicians. We need to either:
  (a) Increase the "unknown" threshold (but that makes REAL SLA breaches slower to detect)
  (b) Build some offline buffer on the mobile app to queue pings and replay them when connectivity returns
  (c) Add a "manual check-in" option that technicians use when exiting connectivity dead zones

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ahmed Hassan [10:28] (c) is a workaround, not a fix. (a) is a product tradeoff that Priya
needs to decide. (b) is offline mode — supposedly out of scope.

But a minimal offline ping buffer is maybe 2 days of work, not the full offline-first
architecture. The mobile app could store the last 20 pings in AsyncStorage and flush them
to the API when connectivity is restored. GPS is still available offline on the device.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Lakshmi Reddy [10:31] Can we get this in front of Priya before sprint 1 locks? This feels
like a scope decision that needs to be made now, not discovered in UAT.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ahmed Hassan [10:32] I'll add it to the sprint 1 planning agenda for tomorrow. Tagging
