SUBJECT: URGENT — FieldOps 2.0 GDPR DPA Amendment — Timeline Confirmation

Priya,

I've completed the initial GDPR impact assessment for the FieldOps 2.0 location tracking feature.
I want to share my findings and be clear about what needs to happen before go-live.

Summary of findings:

1. DPA Amendment: The current Data Processing Agreement with our EU customers does NOT cover
   continuous GPS location tracking of employees. The amendment I've drafted covers:
   - Article 5(1)(e) compliance (30-day retention, explicitly stated)
   - Article 13 transparency obligations (technicians must be notified — mobile app must display
     a clear "Location tracking active" indicator and allow technicians to understand when tracking starts/stops)
   - Cross-border data transfer: location data will transit through AWS eu-west-1 only — must be
     stated explicitly in the DPA amendment

2. Timeline: I need 4 weeks for internal legal review, then the amendment must be sent to customers
   with a 30-day notice period. Realistically, DPA amendment signed by all affected customers = 2026-09-01
   at the earliest. If go-live is 2026-10-15 that is workable — barely.

3. BLOCKER for earlier testing: We cannot run location tracking in UAT with real technician data
   until the amendment is signed. You may use anonymised/synthetic data for QA.

4. ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENT I do not see in the PRD:
   The mobile app MUST display a persistent banner when location tracking is active (per Article 13).
   It cannot be a one-time pop-up that technicians dismiss and forget. The banner must:
   - Be visible on the home screen while location is being tracked
   - State clearly: "Your location is being shared with your dispatcher" (or equivalent)
   - Link to the privacy policy
   This is a new functional requirement, not optional.

5. Kafka topic retention: Your PRD (and technical spec PDF) mentions 7-day default retention for the
   fieldops.technician-location Kafka topic. This must be reduced to 24 hours maximum, per GDPR
   data minimisation principles. Please update TQ-004 to a decision.

Please confirm in writing that the above requirements will be incorporated into the PRD
and that testing will not use real technician location data before DPA amendment is signed.
